Common questions

How accurate is Dezible?
iPhone microphones are good but not laboratory instruments. Readings are typically within a few dB of a reference meter. With Pro you can calibrate Dezible against a reference meter in Settings.

What is dBA?
dBA weights the sound the way human hearing perceives it. It is the standard for noise exposure. Choose plain dB (flat) in the settings menu on the Meter tab if you prefer.

Does Dezible record audio?
No. The microphone is analyzed live and nothing is recorded or uploaded.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Dezible and pick a widget. For the Lock Screen touch and hold the lock screen, tap Customize and add a Dezible widget.

What does Pro cost?
A single $0.99 purchase (price may vary by country) unlocks unlimited history, CSV export, calibration and loud-noise alerts forever. No subscription. Use Restore Purchase in Settings after reinstalling.

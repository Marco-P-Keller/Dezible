Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Dezible collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

Microphone

Dezible uses the microphone only while you are measuring, to calculate the sound level. Audio is analyzed live on your device in memory and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. When you stop measuring or leave the app, the microphone is turned off.

What stays on your device

Your saved measurements (levels over time, no audio), settings and the latest results shown in the widgets are stored locally on your device and in the app's shared container. They are never uploaded and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Dezible does not receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

Dezible contains no advertising and collects nothing, so it is suitable for all ages.

Dezible – Decibel Meter

Measure the noise around you in real time. Dezible turns your iPhone into a sound level meter (dBA) with a live graph, frequency spectrum, safe-exposure guidance, history and Home & Lock Screen widgets.

Features

  • Live decibel meter with A-weighting, Fast and Slow response
  • Min, average, max and peak levels for every measurement
  • Safe listening time for each noise level (85 dBA / 8 hours rule)
  • Live level graph and frequency spectrum
  • History with daily trends and CSV export
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. One-time $0.99 unlocks Pro for life.

Important

Dezible gives an estimate using your iPhone's built-in microphone. It is not a certified measuring instrument and must not be used for legal, medical or occupational safety decisions.
